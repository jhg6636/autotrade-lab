## Objective

Improve the usable yield without changing the flat source-neutral protocol. Candidate selection may
prefer public full text likely to state entry, exit or rebalance, sizing, universe, timing, and data
requirements, but source type, prestige, reported performance, and positive or negative results do
not affect status or later ranking.

## Collection lanes

1. **Directional and allocation rules:** inspect at most ten new public academic or replication
   candidates covering trend, momentum, carry, volatility management, or tactical allocation.
2. **Relative-value and event rules:** inspect at most ten new public academic or replication
   candidates covering mean reversion, statistical arbitrage, seasonality, event, or cross-sectional
   rules.

Each lane must check the current source ledger before inspection, keep admitted evidence packets
disjoint, and report duplicate/repost leads separately without counting them. A collector records
literal facts and ambiguities only; it does not assign final source or hypothesis status.

## Admission and stop conditions

- Admit only retrievable public rule-bearing evidence; do not bypass paywalls or use paid/licensed
  content.
- Do not infer a rule from an abstract, result table, chart, title, or common implementation.
- Stop a lane at ten inspected new unique candidates, even if fewer than ten are admissible.
- Stop and report if a source needs credentials, a license acceptance, schema/classifier changes,
  ranking, backtesting, canonical promotion, Toss access, or any order action.
- Long/short historical rules remain context-only; no executable Korean short-selling application is
  created.

## Evidence packet fields

For each inspected candidate record the source ID, title, authors, stable public URL, publication
version/date, source type/provenance, exact anchors, literal entry, exit/rebalance, sizing, universe,
timeframe/lag, required data, sample, costs/exclusions, ambiguities, and claimed result marked
`unverified`. List only source-stated independently reproducible parameter variants.
