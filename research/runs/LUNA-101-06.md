## Objective

Continue the flat, source-neutral LUNA-101 pool while improving usable-source yield. Candidate
selection may prefer public artifacts that appear to disclose a complete literal rule, but source
type, prestige, claimed performance, and result sign never affect status, weighting, or later rank.
Completeness is an implementation property, not an evidence hierarchy.

The cumulative hypothesis target is already satisfied. This batch therefore favors one faithful
primary mechanism per source and adds a variant only when the source itself fixes a materially
different, independently reproducible rule. Parameter grids, robustness windows, cost assumptions,
subsamples, and performance-selected examples are not separate variants.

## Collection lanes

1. **Pinned reference-rule artifacts:** inspect at most ten new public, version-pinned research
   repositories, institutional rule specifications, replication packages, or executable appendices.
   Prefer artifacts whose inspected version fixes entry, exit or rebalance, sizing, universe,
   timing/lag, and required inputs in one traceable baseline. A configurable toolkit without a
   source-selected baseline is still recorded but remains incomplete.
2. **Literal academic rule tables:** inspect at most ten new public full texts or supplements with
   explicit deterministic equations, algorithms, or rule tables. Prefer calendar, allocation,
   relative-value, breakout, oscillator, and event mechanisms not already represented. Do not
   reconstruct rules from names, charts, result tables, or conventional practice.

Before opening a candidate beyond title/landing-page screening, each collector checks the current
source ledger, the other lane ledger, prior exclusions, and known mirrors. Once the substantive
artifact is inspected, it counts toward the ten-candidate lane cap even if it proves incomplete or
inaccessible. Reposts, mirrors, and alternate URLs for an admitted work are not new candidates.

## Collector deliverables

Each lane writes one evidence packet under `/tmp` and does not edit the repository. For every
inspected candidate include:

- proposed source ID, title, authors/maintainers, public URL, exact version/date, source type, and
  provenance;
- exact page, section, equation, table, file/function, commit, release, or line anchors;
- literal entry, exit/rebalance, sizing, universe, timeframe, execution lag, required inputs, and
  exposure direction;
- fixed parameters, sample, stated costs/exclusions, ambiguities, and claimed results marked
  `unverified`;
- overlap/repost screening result and a conservative collector note identifying every missing rule
  field without assigning final status.

Collectors do not assign usable/incomplete/inaccessible status, create hypotheses, infer relations,
edit staging ledgers, rank sources, or select rules by reported performance. The coordinator verifies
both packets and their SHA-256 hashes before a separate normalization task begins.

## Stop conditions

- Stop before bypassing a paywall, challenge, login, license acceptance, robots restriction, or
  repository permission boundary.
- Stop before downloading or transcribing paid/licensed datasets, credentials, account data, or
  machine-specific secrets.
- Stop rather than infer sizing, an exit, direction, timing, an unstated parameter, or a
  source-selected baseline.
- Stop and report any duplicate ambiguity, source conflict, schema/classifier need, polarity
  conflict, non-deterministic choice, or packet overlap requiring coordinator judgment.
- No ranking, optimization, backtest, canonical promotion, Toss access, live broker action, order,
  or automatic relation merge.
- Historical long/short research remains context-only; no Korean executable short application.

## Completion conditions

- Exactly two disjoint packets contain no more than ten inspected candidates each and disclose all
  inspected outcomes, including incomplete or inaccessible ones.
- Every candidate is new relative to the 80-source starting ledger and has stable public provenance
  or an explicit inaccessible record.
- The coordinator independently audits caps, hashes, duplicates, provenance, and rule-field
  transcription before authorizing normalization.
- Normalization, if later authorized, remains append-only and passes schema/reference, deterministic
  aggregate, canonical-isolation, direction/polarity/exposure, relation, pytest, Ruff, format, and
  diff checks before any PR action.
