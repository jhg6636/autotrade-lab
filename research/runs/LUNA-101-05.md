# LUNA-101-05 — deterministic rule and public replication discovery

- Base integration branch: `integration/LUNA-101-academic-discovery`
- Batch branch: `agent/LUNA-101-05-rule-implementation-batch`
- Starting integration commit: `5a7d7e72e30ab9767ea23a453d2fdc0b596386e3`
- Batch cap: at most 20 new unique candidates across two disjoint lanes
- Stage: evidence collection pending

## Objective

Increase usable-source yield without changing the flat source-neutral protocol. Selection may prefer
public artifacts likely to state deterministic entry, exit/rebalance, sizing, universe, timing, and
data rules, but source type, prestige, claimed performance, and result sign do not affect status or
later ranking.

## Collection lanes

1. **Public replication and implementation artifacts:** inspect at most ten new public academic
   replication papers, institutional replication packages, or version-pinned research-code
   artifacts. A code artifact is admissible only when the inspected public version exposes the
   complete signal, rebalance/exit, sizing, universe, timing, and required inputs; record its exact
   commit, release, or immutable artifact URL when available.
2. **Fixed-rule public academic artifacts:** inspect at most ten new public full texts with literal
   fixed or deterministically enumerable trading rules, favoring underrepresented breakout,
   oscillator, calendar/event, relative-value, and allocation mechanisms. Do not infer a familiar
   implementation from a strategy name.

Before opening a candidate, each collector checks the current source ledger, the other lane ledger,
and disclosed prior-batch exclusions. Reposts, mirrors of an admitted work, and alternative URLs do
not count as new candidates. The two admitted packet sets must be disjoint.

## Evidence packet contract

For every inspected candidate record: proposed unique source ID; title and authors/maintainers;
stable public URL and exact version/date; source type and provenance; exact page, section, equation,
table, file, function, or line anchors; literal entry; exit/rebalance; sizing; universe; timeframe
and lag; required data; sample; costs/exclusions; ambiguities; claimed results marked `unverified`;
and only source-stated independently reproducible mechanisms or finite parameter sets.

Collectors do not assign usable/incomplete/inaccessible status, create hypotheses, decide relations,
edit the repository, or count a repost as independent support. Report every inspected candidate,
including blocked or underspecified items, and stop exactly at the lane cap.

## Stop conditions

- Stop before bypassing a paywall, challenge, login, license acceptance, robots restriction, or
  repository permission boundary.
- Stop before downloading or transcribing paid/licensed datasets, credentials, account data, or
  machine-specific secrets.
- Stop rather than infer a rule from an abstract, README claim, result table, chart, strategy name,
  test expectation, or common implementation.
- Stop and report any schema/classifier change, source conflict, ambiguous direction/polarity,
  non-deterministic parameter choice, or overlap that requires coordinator judgment.
- No ranking, optimization, backtest, canonical promotion, Toss access, live broker action, or order.
- Historical long/short research remains context-only; no Korean executable short application.

## Completion checks

After collection, the coordinator verifies exact caps, evidence hashes, duplicate/repost screening,
public provenance, and packet completeness. A separate implementer then normalizes only admitted
evidence, after which the coordinator reruns append-only, schema/reference, deterministic aggregate,
canonical-isolation, direction/polarity/exposure, relation-suggestion, pytest, Ruff, format, and diff
checks before any PR action.
