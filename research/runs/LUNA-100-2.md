## Selection record

The pilot deliberately uses publicly reachable landing pages or public institutional pages. This is a coverage pilot, not a source ranking. Exact rules were recorded only when visible in the public material; all ten records are `incomplete` because the accessible pages do not expose enough detail for a falsifiable, executable specification. Consequently, the aggregate does not count any of them as usable.

Academic/replication lane:

- [SSRN, Profitability of Momentum Strategies](https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract_id=166840) — momentum profitability and alternative explanations.
- [NBER w8734, The Disposition Effect and Momentum](https://www.nber.org/papers/w8734) — behavioral explanation of momentum.
- [NBER w16747, A Model of Momentum](https://www.nber.org/papers/w16747) — theoretical momentum mechanism.
- [NBER w7032, Pairs Trading](https://www.nber.org/papers/w7032) — distance-based relative-value trading description.
- [arXiv 2109.10662, Dynamic Cointegration-Based Pairs Trading](https://arxiv.org/abs/2109.10662) — dynamic cointegration pairs-trading evaluation.

Korean-market lane:

- [KRX ETF 발행제도](https://regulation.krx.co.kr/contents/RGL/03/03060106/RGL03060106.jsp) — ETF index, asset, and issuance constraints.
- [KRX ETF 결제제도](https://regulation.krx.co.kr/contents/RGL/03/03060102/RGL03060102.jsp) — settlement, NAV, and disclosure inputs.
- [KCMI 국내 개인투자자의 행태적 편의와 거래행태](https://www.kcmi.re.kr/common/downloadw?fgu=002002&fid=25260&fty=004003) — domestic investor behavior context.
- [KCMI 2026년 자산운용산업 주요 이슈](https://www.kcmi.re.kr/publications/pub_detail_view?cno=6715&syear=2026&zcd=002001016&zno=1897) — domestic allocation and rebalancing context.
- [KRX ETF 투자자 공시 및 거래제도 안내](https://regulation.krx.co.kr/contents/RGL/03/03060102/RGL03060102.jsp) — NAV, tracking error, and public ETF information.

The Korean candidates are `kr_equity`/`kr_etf`, `long_only`, nonnegative exposure, and `context_only`; no short rule was made executable. The pilot therefore does not claim Toss readiness or profitability.
